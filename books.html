Books by Charlotte Mary Yonge

This page contains information about Charlotte Mary Yonge’s published works mentioned in her correspondence.

Fiction (by others)

Storehouse of Stories, A Edited by Charlotte M. Yonge. First series

Date: 1870
Publisher: London and New York: Macmillan and Co
Format: Edited book
Serialization:
Illustrator:

This was an anthology of reprinted eighteenth- and early nineteenth-century children’s stories by various hands; a second series appeared in 1872.

The stories were ‘The History of Philip Quarll’, ‘The Renowned History of little Goody Two-Shoes’, ‘The Governess, or, The Little Female Academy’, ‘Jemima Placid, or, The advantage of good-nature’, ‘The Life and Perambulations of a Mouse’, ‘The Village School’, ‘The Little Queen’ and ‘The History of little Jack’.

Non-fiction, biography

'Biographies of Eminent Anglicans'

Date: 1890s
Format: MS Bodleian Library, unpublished

CMY contributed to a series of short biographies of Anglican saints at the behest of the Rev. [J. F. W.

Bullock](../persons.html#221), Rector of Radwinter.

In 1902 he finally published the first three volumes of a projected six volume work called Daily Lections for every Morning and Evening throughout the Year (London: Rivington 1902).

Volume V of this work, described in his preface, was to have consisted of 'Lections on Anglican Worthies, arranged as additional Readings for Sunday'.

These 'Biographies of Eminent Anglicans' exist in MS and are catalogued under that name among the Bullock papers in the Bodleian Library, Oxford, but were never published, since no more of the Daily Lections ever appeared.
